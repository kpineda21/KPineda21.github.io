Dashboards I've built

Selected pages from interactive Power BI dashboards I designed and maintain for Stockton Unified School District. All figures shown are school- and district-level aggregates approved for public sharing.

State Assessment Participation Tracker

Power BI · 21-page interactive report

A live tracker used by district leadership through testing season — participation gauges for every state assessment (ELPAC, CAASPP, CAA, PFT) against California's 95% requirement, with drill-downs by school and director and year-over-year comparisons.

CAASPP Preliminary Results Explorer

Power BI · 11-page interactive report

Summarizes ELA and math achievement for roughly 19,000 students — grade-level breakdowns, achievement-level distributions, average distance from standard, and four-year trends, filterable by school, grade, and English-learner status.

ELPAC Summative Results Explorer

Power BI · 4-page interactive report

Tracks English-language proficiency results for roughly 6,500 English learners — performance levels across the listening, speaking, reading, and writing domains, grade-level detail, and four-year trends, filterable by school, grade, special education, and 504 status.
